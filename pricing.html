Questions

Do I need a card to start?

No. The Free plan needs no card — sign up and start optimizing right away.

What happens if I hit my monthly limit?

You'll see a prompt to upgrade. Your existing history and account stay untouched — you just won't be able to run new optimizes or verifies until the next cycle or an upgrade.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, cancel anytime from your Account page. You'll keep paid features until the end of your billing period.
