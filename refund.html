Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 2026

We want you to be happy with Promptscaper. This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for our Starter and Pro subscription plans.

1. Free Plan

Our Free plan requires no payment, so no refund applies. You can use it for as long as you like within its monthly limits.

2. Cancelling a Paid Plan

You can cancel your Starter or Pro subscription at any time from your Account page. When you cancel:

  • You will continue to have access to your paid plan's features until the end of your current billing cycle.
  • You will not be charged again after cancellation.
  • Your account will automatically move to the Free plan once the billing cycle ends.

3. Refunds

  • Accidental or duplicate charges: If you were charged more than once for the same billing cycle, or charged in error, contact us within 7 days and we will issue a full refund.
  • Service not delivered: If a technical issue on our end prevented you from using your paid plan's features for a significant part of the billing cycle, contact us — we will review and may issue a partial or full refund at our discretion.
  • Change of mind: Since paid plans grant immediate access to higher usage limits, we generally do not offer refunds for the current billing cycle if you simply change your mind after paying. You can still cancel to avoid future charges, as described above.

4. How to Request a Refund

Email us at promptscaper.india@gmail.com with your registered email address and the reason for your request. We aim to respond within 3–5 business days. Approved refunds are processed back to your original payment method via Razorpay and may take 5–7 business days to reflect, depending on your bank.
