Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Promptscaper ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website and Service.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name and email address when you sign up.
  • Content you submit: the rough prompts you optimize and the AI answers you paste in to verify.
  • Usage data: how many optimizations/verifications you've used, and which plan you're on.
  • Payment information: if you subscribe to a paid plan, payment is processed by Razorpay. We do not store your card or UPI details ourselves.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the prompt-optimization and answer-verification features you request.
  • To manage your account, plan, and monthly usage limits.
  • To send important account or billing communications.
  • To improve the Service and fix issues.

3. Sharing With Third-Party Services

To provide the Service, the text you submit is shared with the following processors, only for the purpose of generating your result:

  • Anthropic (Claude API) — to optimize prompts and analyze claims.
  • Tavily — to run web searches when verifying claims.
  • Supabase — to store your account, plan, and usage data securely.
  • Razorpay — to process subscription payments.

We do not sell your personal data to advertisers or other third parties.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account and usage data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

5. Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by emailing us. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures (including encrypted storage and access controls) to protect your data. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to safeguard your information.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
